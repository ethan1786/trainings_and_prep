Why it works at launch:
  • Single deploy = fast iteration
  • Shared DB = simple consistency
  • Team is small, co-located
  • Zero operational overhead
Why we split (triggers):
  • Lead capture needs independent SLA
  • Inventory sync tolerates eventual consistency
  • Teams growing — ownership boundaries
  • Deploy coupling slows releases
Mature trade-offs accepted:
  • Operational complexity (observability needed)
  • Eventual consistency (conflict resolution)
  • Network partitions (circuit breakers)
  • Data duplication across services

Part 2: Payment & Reservation Subsystem

Zoom into the payment flow within Stage 3

Component Architecture

Reservation State Machine

Why auth-then-capture?
  • Cancel before capture is free (no refund needed)
  • Dealer confirms deal before money moves
  • Reduces refund friction and disputes
  • Deposit holds immediately, charges later
Why event-sourced actions?
  • Full audit trail of every state change
  • Reconcile with payment provider anytime
  • Idempotent webhook handling (replay-safe)
  • Linked-list gives ordering guarantee
Why async + webhook?
  • Don't block user on payment settlement
  • Handle provider timeouts gracefully
  • Reconciliation worker catches stuck txns
  • Scales independently of checkout traffic
