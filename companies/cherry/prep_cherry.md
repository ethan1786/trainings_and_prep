## Key Themes Across All Rounds

Cherry is a startup looking for generalists who can own features end-to-end. Every round should reinforce:

1. **Full-stack ownership** — you build frontend, backend, and infrastructure, not just one layer
2. **Startup mentality** — you've been early at a company before (Toast), you know how to ship fast and iterate
3. **Collaboration** — cross-team, cross-functional, not a solo operator
4. **Product sense** — you understand why you're building something, not just how

## Concerns to Address Naturally (don't bring up, but have answers ready)

- **React gap:** Frame Angular/TypeScript as directly transferable. Component-based, TypeScript, same patterns. "I'd be productive in React within a week."
- **Java-heavy background for a Node/TS shop:** Highlight Applause (Angular + Node.js BFF full-stack) and Toast (jQuery/Angular + backend). You've done this before.
- **Private company risk:** If asked what concerns you, be honest but constructive. "I'm evaluating total comp holistically — the equity story matters to me."
