## Interview Process

Below you will find a high-level overview of the process in addition to more detailed insight into each step. If you have any questions as you progress through the process, please feel free to reach out to your Recruiter or Hiring Manager. We’re here to support you (and cheer you on 🥳)!

---

### Overview

1. Technical Recruiting Screen - 30 minutes
2. Hiring Manager Interview - 60 minutes
3. Technical Rounds
    1. Coding Interview - 60 minutes
    2. System Design Interview - 60 minutes
    3. Application Design Interview - 60 minutes
4. References x 3
5. Final Round - 75 minutes

## The Interviews

### Technical Recruiting Screen

The technical recruiting screen is an initial call with the hiring manager or recruiter to explore whether there’s a strong mutual fit for the role. We’ll spend time learning about your background, your career journey, what’s motivating your search, and what draws you to Cherry. You’ll also have the opportunity to learn more about us, the role, and ask any questions you have! We want this to be a helpful, informative conversation for you as well.

---

### Hiring Manager Interview

The hiring manager Interview is an opportunity for us to learn more about how you work as an engineer within a real team and business environment—beyond purely technical execution. This conversation helps us understand how you **collaborate, make decisions, balance technical excellence with business needs, navigate challenges, and grow in a professional setting**. 

We’ll talk through your **past experiences, including projects you've worked on, challenges you've faced, and decisions you've made in a team setting**. We may ask follow-up questions to help us better understand how you approach collaboration with cross-functional partners, take ownership of problems end-to-end, and navigate differing perspectives.

Just as importantly, this is dedicated time for you to connect with the hiring manager! You’ll have space at the end to ask questions, learn more about what we're building here at Cherry, and the role you'll have in that growth.

⭐️ Strong conversations here tend to include thoughtful reflection on past experiences, with clear examples that show how you approach different situations. We’re excited to learn how you think and what you’ve learned along the way!

### Coding Interview

Our coding interview is purely backend and supports the following languages**: Python, Java, Kotlin, Golang, Ruby,  C# and TypeScript**, and this interview will be conducted through Coderpad.

***Note: Please let your recruiter and hiring manager know of your preference in language ahead of the interview.***

We focus on problem-solving skills, logical thinking, core coding fundamentals and some basic language-specific fluency. Feel free to be scrappy in the interview & call out how you would do it in production so we know how well you know the language specific nuances!

You may use external resources during the interview, including AI, for help with language syntax, standard libraries, or small implementation details. CoderPad provides an inbuilt ChatGPT interface—please use this if you choose to use AI.

That said, ask that you please refrain from using AI to solve the problem end-to-end, as this exercise is meant to help us understand your own problem-solving approach.

Please walk us through your thinking, assumptions, and approach before and during implementation. We ask that you please write the code primarily yourself, using AI only as a supporting tool along the way, rather than as a replacement for your own reasoning.

**⭐️ Tips for Success:**

- Take a moment to map out your approach, whether in pseudocode or by talking it through, before jumping into implementation.
- Start by getting a working solution in place, then iterate toward a more optimal approach.

### System Design

Our System Design interview is a whiteboarding session focused on **infrastructure** and **high-level system design**. We’ll discuss topics like load balancers, inter-service communication, and scaling.

This session uses Coderpad, which includes [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com/) for whiteboarding. The link will be shared with you in the session, but feel free to play around with the platform ahead of time to get comfortable!

We recommend creating a coherent diagram and actively engage with the interviewer throughout, exploring details in depth, discussing tradeoffs, and defending your design decisions.

**⭐️ Tips for Success:**

- Take time to clarify requirements upfront to ensure you’re solving the right problem
- Start with a simple, working end-to-end solution, then iterate and refine as needed
- Talk through tradeoffs as you make decisions so we can better understand your thinking

*Please note that outside resources are not permitted for the system design interview. This session is more about your critical thinking and fundamentals.*

---

### Application Design

Our Application Design interview is a whiteboarding session focused on **modeling data in the database, API interface design, frontend client considerations**, and **product sense**. We’ll look for you to write pseudo-code data models in your preferred language or system and write high-level Swagger/OpenAPI specs for endpoints.

This session uses Coderpad, which includes [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com/) for whiteboarding. The link will be shared with you in the session, but feel free to play around with the platform ahead of time to get comfortable!

We recommend representing all entities and concepts in your diagram, and iterating on your design as new product requirements emerge throughout the session.

**⭐️ Tips for Success:**

- Take time to clarify requirements upfront to ensure you’re solving the right problem
- Start with a simple, working end-to-end solution, then iterate and refine as needed
- Talk through tradeoffs as you make decisions so we can better understand your thinking

### Final Round

The final step in our process is a 75-minute conversation with a few members of our leadership team (including our wonderful CTO & Co-Founder, Charles!). It's genuinely one of the more personal parts of our process: we love getting to know candidates as whole people, not just as a set of skills, and hearing the story of how you got to where you are today.

We’ll walk through your journey together — from your early academic years through your current role — talking through what’s motivated your choices throughout your career, what you’ve learned, what you're proud of, and how you approach new challenges. We recommend approaching this session by knowing your story well through each chapter and being able to point to specific examples of your impact along the way (*think: objective data points & quantified impact: e.g., "increased revenue by X%" or "improved satisfaction scores from 3.9 to 4.6"*).

Above all, we’re looking for clear, thoughtful communication and a genuine reflection of how you’ve grown throughout your career. We’re excited to learn more about your story!
