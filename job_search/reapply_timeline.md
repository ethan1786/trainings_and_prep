## Rules for This Phase

1. **Apply when you spot a fit. Don't batch or schedule.**
2. **10 minutes per application max.** Resume + submit. No custom cover letters.
3. **Don't prep until you have a scheduled round.** Focus on side project.
4. **If someone responds, take the call relaxed.** IBM energy. Conversational. No cramming.
5. **One habit to practice:** "I need X. Options are A vs B. Picking A because reason."
